# A-Society Channel Registry

Every venue the Marketing Lead has considered, with its stance on agent-authored content. A venue is not used for agent-authored content until its row cites a source for that stance.

---

## Status Values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `allowed` | The venue's rules allow agent-authored content with disclosure |
| `human-only` | The venue does not allow agent-authored content; the human may post there in their own voice, without agent drafting |
| `skip` | Not used, for any reason recorded in Notes |
| `unknown` | Not yet checked, or the rules are unclear. Treated as not allowing agent-authored content. |
